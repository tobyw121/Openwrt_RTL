Hardware evidence used from the v43.8 serial capture
-----------------------------------------------------
- Physical LAN remains RTL8367 port 1.
- A real switch-to-CPU RX frame reached the RTL8197F DMA buffer with:
    EtherType 0x8899, second tag word 0x0401, encapsulated IPv6 0x86dd.
- v43.8 rejected that frame as "RX unknown protocol=0x0 tag=0x0401".
- CPU-to-switch TX for LAN reached the pre-DMA trace with 0x8899/0x9202.
- Therefore RX and TX second-word layouts are treated as directional/asymmetric.
- The v43.8 log did not directly capture a raw WAN 0x0403 word. v43.9 decodes
  the low six source-port bits generically after the observed 0x0400 marker.

v43.9 source corrections
------------------------
- Driver version: 1.4.9-sdk-mw5-rxtag-ring-v43.9.
- tag_rtl4_9 RX recognizes the observed switch-to-CPU 0x0400 marker layout.
- tag_rtl4_9 TX keeps protocol 9 / learn-disable / destination-mask encoding.
- Real 0x8899 RX frames pass through the master unchanged.
- Descriptor fallback synthesizes 0x0400|source-port, not the TX protocol-9 word.
- MW5 RX descriptor OWN is authoritative; CPURPDCR0 cannot override OWN.
- RX recycle restores desc->addr from the software-owned rx_dma[] mapping.
- Bounded RX descriptor address/opts/CDP diagnostics added.
- Bounded TX submit/completion/CDP/OWN diagnostics added.
- mw5-netdiag waits for normal netifd LAN/br-lan readiness before autotest.
- Standard OpenWrt firewall4 policy remains enabled/unchanged from v43.8.
