RTL8197F OpenWrt 24.10 v42.5 validation
========================================

Validated source properties:
- MW5 uses a 16-byte Realtek IMG_HEADER_T.
- MW5 does not enable length-includes-header-padding.
- AC23 retains its separate 60-byte reconstructed format.
- MW5 generated OpenWrt firmware is rejected if its payload begins with the
  obsolete 40-byte OEM prefix.
- Board-profile, patch, WLAN-variant, firmware-budget and private-template
  validation remain enabled.

Validated transformed user image:
- full size: 0x800000
- firmware window: 0x030000..0x5c0000
- old payload length: 0x2b3110
- new payload length: 0x2b30e8
- loader moved from firmware+0x38 to firmware+0x10
- rootfs moved from 0x2e3120 to 0x2e30f8
- kernel additive checksum: 0x0000
- rootfs additive checksum: 0x0000
- ranges 0x000000..0x030000 and 0x5c0000..0x800000 unchanged

Hardware status:
- Exact image structure is verified offline.
- UART boot on the physical MW5 is still required to confirm execution beyond
  the LZMA loader and expose any subsequent platform issue.
