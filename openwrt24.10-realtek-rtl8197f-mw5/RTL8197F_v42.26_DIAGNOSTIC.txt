RTL8197F MW5 v42.26 diagnostic

Observed v42.25 hardware state:
- external switch ID 0x6367 revision 0x0060 detected
- LAN and WAN PHY links reach 1 Gbit/s

- eth0 RX stays at zero
- TX descriptors time out with NETDEV WATCHDOG
- CPUICR1 readback is 0x000001d3: descriptor stride fields are zero
- DMA_CR1 is 0x000007e0: Linux configured 63 * 32 bytes

SDK correlation:
- CONFIG_RTL_TX_CACHE_ALIGN and CONFIG_RTL_RX_CACHE_ALIGN are defined only

- native new descriptor is therefore 6 dwords / 24 bytes

- VG/RD05 desc_dwords=8, desc_stride=32
- DMA_CR1 uses runtime stride
- CPURPDCR0/CPUTPDCR0 and CPUICR1 are rearmed after CPUIF is active
