RD05 v5ai extpair diagnostic
============================

Base: v5ah/v5ag/v5af/v5ab.

Purpose:
  v5ag/v5ah showed that RX mirror to physical port 7 and to physical port 6
  does not make PC ARP appear in RTL8197F DMA RX, while lan2 broadcast MIBs grow
  and dot1dTpPortInDiscards stays zero.

Change:
  Keep DSA CPU mask on CPU7 (0x80), but put both physical EXT candidates 6 and 7
  into the diagnostic external RTL8367D VID9/flood/isolation domain:
    extpair=0xc0 plus user ports => 0xdf

Expected marker:
  dsa-tag=rtl8_4/8byte/cpu7-extpair-v5ai
  extpair=0xdf
  oemmask=0xdf
  flood u/m/b=0xdf/0xdf/0xdf
  iso1/6/7=0xdf/0xdf/0xdf

Not compile-tested and not hardware-tested in this environment.
