[RTL839x/5xM,RTL838x/3xM] Fix EEE and Diag(RTCT) modules' semaphore issue.
Symptom:
		(1) EEE uses MAC polling PHY and PHY functions, that will be conflict with PORT module.
		(2) Diag's RTCT function uses PHY function, that will be conflict with PORT module.
		These operations lack for protection when the multiple modules access the same low layer resource.
Solution:
		Port, EEE and Diag modules use PORT semaphore.
